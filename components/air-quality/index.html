What it measures

The air component uses the current peak Air Quality Index reported by the closest AirNow reporting area. AirNow recommends its nationwide file products for maintaining a database of reporting-area observations. This is outdoor ambient air, not an indoor-air measurement.

Interpretation rule: HomeNHealthy scores geographic context. None of these components should replace property-specific testing, inspection or professional advice.
